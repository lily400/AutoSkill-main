---END OF THE GAME AND FINAL SCORING 
The end of the game is triggered when at least 1 of the 3 possible end conditions is met at the end of a turn. 
The 3 end conditions - 1 You have supported 4 Conservation objectives, so all 4 of your Conservation Achievement markers are on the Conservation board. 2 You covered your Zoo map completely, so there is not a single empty space left on it. 3 The pile of face-down tiles is empty. If, at the end of your turn, you've met condition 1 and/or 2 take the 10-point End-of-game marker. Then everyone else gets 1 more turn (excluding you). After that, move on to the final scoring. If the pile of face-down tiles is empty after you have triggered the end of the game, shuffle all the discarded tiles to form a new pile. □ If you also meet condition 1 and/or 2 on your last turn, after someone else has triggered the end of the game, take a 5-point End-of-game marker. If you meet condition 3 before one of you meets condition 1 or 2, the game ends immediately after your turn. Directly proceed to the final scoring. 
Final scoring Add up: • the points on the tiles in your zoo. Some tiles have a fixed point value. For other tiles, the value depends on how many icons of a specific type you have adjacent to that tile or anywhere in your zoo.
